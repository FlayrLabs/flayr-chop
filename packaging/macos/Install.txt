Installing Flayr Chop on macOS
==============================

1. Drag "Flayr Chop.component" onto "Drag AU here".
   (Logic Pro, GarageBand, MainStage and other Audio Unit hosts.)

2. Drag "Flayr Chop.vst3" onto "Drag VST3 here".
   (Ableton Live, FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper, Bitwig and other VST3 hosts.)

   macOS asks for your password because these are the shared plug-in folders.
   You can install either one or both.

3. Restart your DAW, or rescan plug-ins:
   - Logic Pro: Settings > Plug-in Manager > Reset & Rescan Selection if it doesn't appear.
     It is an instrument: Software Instrument track > Instrument slot > AU Instruments > Flayr Labs > Flayr Chop.
   - Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-Ins > turn on "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders" > Rescan.
   - FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins.
   - Reaper: Preferences > Plug-ins > VST > Re-scan.

The plug-ins are signed and notarized by Flayr Labs LLC.
Source code, help and updates: https://github.com/FlayrLabs/flayr-chop
